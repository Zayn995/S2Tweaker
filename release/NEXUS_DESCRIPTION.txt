[heading]WHAT IT DOES[/heading]

A small Windows tool with sliders and checkboxes. You pick your values, it builds a ready-to-use .pak mod from them. Everything is a multiplier or toggle on top of the real values read from your game. Around [b]160 tweaks[/b], sorted into 8 tabs (Player, Weight & items, Combat, NPCs & AI, Weapons, Ammo, World, Economy):

[b]Player[/b]
[list]
[*]Health, stamina (incl. per-action costs), fall damage, jump height, breath hold
[*]Walk/crouch and run/sprint speed as separate sliders
[*]Max carry weight, overweight penalty start, item weights per category
[*]Radiation, bleeding, hunger & sleepiness, headshot multiplier, explosion damage
[*]Hit camera shake / aim punch 0-300%
[/list]

[b]Weapons - a three-level system[/b]
[list]
[*]8 parameters, each on three levels: [b]global[/b] sliders, [b]8 category[/b] factors (pistols, SMGs, assault rifles, shotguns, DMRs, snipers, machine guns, launchers) and [b]per-weapon overrides for 79 weapons[/b] incl. all uniques - open a category, open a weapon, set its factors. Single weapon beats category, category beats global, everything left at (vanilla) falls through
[*]The 8 parameters: damage, spread, recoil, durability, fire rate, effective range, bleeding (0% = your bullets never cause it), ADS movement speed
[*]Magazine size - scales weapon base capacity and all 81 magazine attachments
[*]Melee damage (knife & rifle butt strike)
[*]Jamming, scoped aim sway (without breaking the offset/lean aiming)
[*]The tool resolves the game's real weapon links and tells you when a weapon shares stats with its variants
[/list]

[b]Ammo - its own tab, two levels[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Global[/b] sliders for damage, armor piercing, armor damage and cover penetration - scaled per ammo type, so AP stays the armor king and buckshot stays bad at it, just more or less extreme
[*][b]Per-round overrides[/b] for all [b]34 rounds in 14 calibers[/b] (9x18, 9x19, .45, 9x39, 5.45, 5.56, 7.62x39, 7.62 NATO, 7.62x54R, 12 gauge, gauss, VOG-25, 40 mm HEDP, PG-7V): open a caliber, open a round, set its factors - a round's own factor beats the global slider, everything left at (vanilla) falls through
[*]Only values that can actually be scaled get a slider: many rounds have armor piercing and cover penetration at 0 in vanilla, and 0 x anything is still 0
[/list]

[b]Armor[/b]
[list]
[*]Protection per damage type: physical, burn, shock, chemical, radiation, PSY - scales YOUR armor only, NPC armor stays vanilla, upgrade bonuses untouched
[*]Armor durability (separate from weapon durability)
[*]Armor & exoskeleton carry-weight bonuses 0-300% (0% = armor grants no extra carry weight)
[/list]

[b]NPCs & AI[/b]
[list]
[*]NPC damage, health and accuracy
[*]Vision and hearing range down to 10% - combine both for ghost-mode stealth
[*]Grenade usage (0% = no more grenade spam) and reaction delay (how long NPCs take to alert their squad)
[*]"NPCs don't self-heal" - vanilla NPCs passively regenerate health while you regenerate none
[*]Experimental A-Life sliders: max simultaneous NPCs & mutants, spawn distance
[/list]

[b]Mutants[/b]
[list]
[*]Global mutant damage, health, speed and hearing sliders
[*]Per-species overrides via dropdown: health, speed and damage for every species - damage scales each attack individually (claw / jump / charge ...)
[*]Bloodsucker cloaking: cloaking speed, and how hard damage breaks the cloak (x0 = hits never reveal them)
[/list]

[b]Artifacts & detectors[/b]
[list]
[*]Artifact effect strength, radiation side-effect (0% = clean artifacts), spawn chance
[*]Rare artifact bias: shift rarity rolls toward Uncommon/Rare/Epic at Common's expense
[*]Detector & scanner range: Echo, Bear, Veles, Gilka, the anomaly beeper and the searchpoint scanner
[/list]

[b]World[/b]
[list]
[*]Anomaly damage - one global slider plus per element type: electro / chemical / fire / gravity
[*]Consumable strength: medkits, bandages, food & drinks (penalties like drunkenness stay vanilla)
[*]Rain & storm frequency, emission frequency
[*][b]Loot[/b] - two separate systems, four sliders: the smart-loot lists in stashes and on bodies (amount, find chance, ammo bonus), and the item generators behind dead stalkers, mutants and the Zone's stashes (amount). Money, quest items, story rewards, unique weapons and trader stock are excluded by design
[/list]

[b]Economy[/b]
[list]
[*]Trader buy/sell prices, minimum durability traders accept, per-category price factors (weapons, armor, ammo, artifacts, consumables)
[*]Repair & upgrade costs, quest rewards
[*]Fast travel cost 0-400% (0% = guides are free), trader restock time
[/list]

[b]Tool features[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Save & load presets[/b] (JSON files in a presets folder next to the exe)
[*][b]Search box[/b] to find any slider - highlights matches, shows which tab, and also finds weapons and ammo rounds by name (opening their category for you)
[*]Reads the vanilla values [b]from YOUR installed game version[/b], so multiplier tweaks stay correct after game patches
[*][b]Only the values you CHANGE get patched[/b] - everything left on "(vanilla)" is untouched, so it won't conflict with your other mods
[/list]

[heading]HOW TO USE (step by step)[/heading]

[list=1]
[*]Download and extract the ZIP
[*]Create a folder, for example on your desktop: Desktop\S2Tweaker
[*]Put S2Tweaker.exe (and README.txt) into that folder
[*]Run S2Tweaker.exe
[*]The tool suggests your game folder, e.g. C:\Games\Steam\steamapps\common\S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2 Heart of Chornobyl - check it. If it's wrong, click "Browse ..." and select your game folder (the one that contains Stalker2\Content\Paks)
[*]Click "Confirm & load game data" and wait 10-20 seconds (only needed on first start and after game updates)
[*]Set your sliders and checkboxes. Everything you leave at "(vanilla)" will NOT be part of the mod
[*]Optional: change the mod name at the bottom
[*]Click "Build pak -> output folder"
[*]Open Desktop\S2Tweaker\output - there is your mod, e.g. zzz_S2Tweaker_P.pak
[*]Copy that file into: <your game folder>\Stalker2\Content\Paks\~mods\
Example: C:\Games\Steam\steamapps\common\S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2 Heart of Chornobyl\Stalker2\Content\Paks\~mods\
If the ~mods folder doesn't exist, create it.
(Shortcut: the "Install to ~mods" button does steps 9-11 in one click and creates the ~mods folder automatically)
[*]Start the game. Done!
[/list]

[heading]CHANGE OR REMOVE THE MOD[/heading]

[list]
[*]Change values: move the sliders, build again - the new pak replaces the old one (same mod name = same file name)
[*]Remove the mod: delete zzz_<YourModName>_P.pak from the ~mods folder, or click "Remove from ~mods" in the tool
[*]Uninstall the tool: just delete your S2Tweaker folder - settings, cache and output all live inside it (fully portable, nothing else is written anywhere)
[/list]

[heading]GOOD TO KNOW[/heading]

[list]
[*]After a game update: start the tool and confirm the game folder again - it detects the new version and re-reads the vanilla values automatically. Then rebuild your pak
[*]If you already use single-purpose mods that change the same values (e.g. "carry weight 200"), remove them - this tool covers them and its pak loads last anyway
[*]Weapon fire rate, spread and recoil live in game data shared with NPCs - NPCs using those weapons are affected too (noted in the app)
[*]Since game patch 2.0 the community reports that cfg-based movement-speed changes can desync from animations or partially not apply - the movement and carry-weight sliders carry an in-app warning until this settles
[*]Some antivirus tools flag freshly built Python exes - this is a known false-positive pattern
[*]Reading the game's packed config files needs Oodle's oo2core_9_win64.dll (0.6 MB). It is proprietary and cannot be shipped with the tool (and the game does not ship it as a separate file either), so S2Tweaker downloads it once from the public OodleUE mirror on GitHub, verifies its official checksum and keeps it in a "tools" folder next to the exe - after that everything works offline. If antivirus HTTPS inspection, a proxy or a VPN blocks github.com, simply put that DLL next to S2Tweaker.exe yourself and the tool will use it (it has to be the exact build repak expects - the tool tells you if the checksum does not match)
[*]Debug checkbox: also exports the raw .cfg patch files to output\<ModName>_cfg so you can see exactly what the mod changes
[/list]

[heading]CHANGELOG[/heading]

[b]v1.7.0 - Loot amounts[/b] (~160 sliders)
[list]
[*][b]Stashes & bodies[/b] - three sliders for the game's smart-loot lists: how much a stash or body yields, the chance a slot yields anything at all, and the bonus ammo handed out to match a found weapon's caliber. Honest limits, so you know what to expect: those lists hold ammo, medicine, food and grenades - no weapons, armor or artifacts - and vanilla only puts smart loot on bodies on Easy and Medium difficulty
[*][b]Loot amount (NPCs, containers, world)[/b] - the game's other, much larger loot system: the item generators behind dead stalkers and mutants and behind the stashes scattered around the Zone. The slider changes how many of an item a slot yields, not how often you find something and not which items show up
[*]What it deliberately does NOT touch: coupons (neither a generator's money block nor the money cards lying in stashes), quest items, story rewards, unique weapons, and trader stock - a named trader's shelves stay vanilla. Amounts are whole numbers and never drop below 1, so a slot that yields a single item only changes from 150 % upward
[*]The safety filter checks every single item a generator can produce against the game's own item database (both quest markers) - 2,010 of the game's 3,085 loot generators pass it, the rest is left alone
[*]Heads-up: this is by far the largest patch the tool can build (~24,000 lines). If the game starts noticeably slower afterwards, put that slider back to 100 %
[*]Also fixed: loading a preset now resets everything to vanilla first, so sliders that did not exist when the preset was saved can no longer sneak into your pak; a failed or incomplete game-data extraction is detected instead of leaving a broken cache behind; caches from older tool versions are deleted instead of piling up
[*]The tool now extracts two more game files, so the first start after updating takes a few seconds longer than usual
[/list]

[b]v1.6.1 - Bug fix: "Oodle loader error ... InvalidCertificate(UnknownIssuer)"[/b]
[list]
[*]If loading the game data failed with that error, this version fixes it - thanks to foxce for the report. Reading the game's config files needs Oodle's oo2core_9_win64.dll; until now the bundled repak fetched it and validated the connection against its own certificate list instead of the Windows one, so antivirus/proxy HTTPS inspection broke it. It was also stored in a temp folder that Windows wipes, so it was re-downloaded on every cold start
[*]S2Tweaker now gets that library itself (Windows certificate store), verifies its official checksum, prefers a copy already on your PC and keeps it in a "tools" folder next to the exe - fetched once, offline afterwards. If your network blocks GitHub, just drop the DLL next to S2Tweaker.exe
[*]If it still can't be obtained you now get a plain explanation with those steps instead of a Python error
[*]No tweaks changed in this version
[/list]
