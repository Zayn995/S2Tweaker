[heading]WHAT IT DOES[/heading]

A small Windows tool with sliders and checkboxes. You pick your values, it builds a ready-to-use .pak mod from them. Everything is a multiplier or toggle on top of the real values read from your game. Around [b]240 tweaks[/b], sorted into 13 tabs (Player, Vaulting, Weight & items, Combat, NPCs & AI, Mutants, Factions, Weapons, Ammo, Armor, World, Economy, Traders):

[b]Player[/b]
[list]
[*]Health (up to 100000 on a logarithmic slider), stamina (incl. per-action costs), fall damage, jump height, breath hold
[*]Walk/crouch and run/sprint speed as separate sliders
[*]Max carry weight, overweight penalty start, item weights per category, 'quest items weigh nothing'
[*]Radiation, bleeding, hunger & sleepiness, headshot multiplier, explosion damage
[*]Hit camera shake / aim punch 0-300%
[*]Interaction reach (pick up, loot, containers) and talk distance for NPC dialogs; saving: manual, quick and autosave slot limits plus the autosave interval
[/list]

[b]Vaulting - its own tab[/b]
[list]
[*]Seven sliders for how Skif climbs and vaults: max vault height, trigger distance, approach angle, min obstacle height, landing tolerance, vault-over thickness and vault-over landing distance
[*]'Improved vaulting' preset restores the tuned set of the vault mod that broke with game patch 2.0 - the sliders stack on top of it
[*]Experimental 'start vaulting while sprinting' toggle (effect not verified yet - feedback welcome)
[*]Player only - NPCs keep vanilla vaulting. The game's internal trace values (search radii, detection iterations) stay in the preset on purpose: as sliders they would be unexplainable
[/list]

[b]Weapons - a three-level system[/b]
[list]
[*]10 parameters, each on three levels: [b]global[/b] sliders, [b]8 category[/b] factors (pistols, SMGs, assault rifles, shotguns, DMRs, snipers, machine guns, launchers) and [b]per-weapon overrides for 91 weapons[/b] incl. all unique named guns (Decider, Sharpshooter, Sotnyk ...) and the Deluxe/Ultimate/Pre-order edition guns - open a category, open a weapon, set its factors. Single weapon beats category, category beats global, everything left at (vanilla) falls through
[*]Weapons show their real in-game names next to the internal ID ("GunAK74_ST · AKM-74S") and the search box finds both spellings
[*]The 10 parameters: damage, spread, recoil, durability, fire rate, effective range, bleeding (0% = your bullets never cause it), ADS movement speed, ADS aim-in speed (how fast the weapon comes up into the sights), magazine size
[*]Magazine size on all three levels - scales a weapon's base capacity and the magazines that weapon really uses (read from its own reload table); the global slider covers all 80 magazine attachments
[*][b]Recoil reduction from upgrades[/b]: boosts the recoil-reduction upgrades and attachments (vanilla -5 % to -30 %, capped at -100 %) - at 2000 % any recoil upgrade removes the kick entirely. Only weapons with such an upgrade installed change; community-proven on patch 2.0
[*]Melee damage and melee range (knife & rifle butt strike)
[*]Jamming, scoped aim sway (without breaking the offset/lean aiming)
[*]The tool resolves the game's real weapon links and tells you when a weapon shares stats with its variants
[/list]

[b]Ammo - its own tab, two levels[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Global[/b] sliders for damage, armor piercing, armor damage and cover penetration - scaled per ammo type, so AP stays the armor king and buckshot stays bad at it, just more or less extreme
[*][b]Per-round overrides[/b] for all [b]34 rounds in 14 calibers[/b] (9x18, 9x19, .45, 9x39, 5.45, 5.56, 7.62x39, 7.62 NATO, 7.62x54R, 12 gauge, gauss, VOG-25, 40 mm HEDP, PG-7V): open a caliber, open a round, set its factors - a round's own factor beats the global slider, everything left at (vanilla) falls through
[*]Only values that can actually be scaled get a slider: many rounds have armor piercing and cover penetration at 0 in vanilla, and 0 x anything is still 0
[/list]

[b]Armor - its own tab, two levels[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Global[/b] protection sliders per damage type: physical, burn, shock, chemical, radiation, PSY - scales YOUR armor only, NPC armor stays vanilla, upgrade bonuses untouched
[*][b]Per-armor overrides for all 57 armors and helmets[/b] incl. the edition pieces, listed under their real in-game names: open Body armor or Helmets, open the piece ("Cuirass Exoskeleton", "SEVA Suit" ...), set its protection factors - a piece's own factor beats the global slider, everything left at (vanilla) falls through. Only protection types the piece actually has get a slider
[*]Armor durability (separate from weapon durability)
[*]Armor & exoskeleton carry-weight bonuses 0-300% (0% = armor grants no extra carry weight)
[/list]

[b]NPCs & AI[/b]
[list]
[*]NPC damage, health and accuracy
[*]Vision and hearing range down to 10% - combine both for ghost-mode stealth
[*]Grenade usage (0% = no more grenade spam) and reaction delay (how long NPCs take to alert their squad)
[*]"NPCs don't self-heal" - vanilla NPCs passively regenerate health while you regenerate none
[*][b]NPC gear quality[/b]: tilts the random loadout pools toward the expensive end (or the cheap end, below 100 %) - the game's own price data is the quality rank. Honest limit: it only re-weights what a pool already contains, it never adds or removes items
[*][b]NPC combat behaviour[/b] (experimental): the hidden per-weapon AI profiles behind 'aimbot' complaints - guaranteed-hit shots per burst (0 % = every NPC shot uses normal spread), burst length, fire pauses, engagement range, NPC weapon range and NPC health regen as a factor
[*][b]Stealth[/b] (experimental): crouch stealth, movement noise, bad-weather stealth (how much fog, rain and thunder blind and deafen NPCs) and how strongly your own flashlight gives you away
[*][b]NPC awareness & nerve[/b] (experimental): alertness thresholds, search time, courage (attack / fall-back confidence), stagger threshold, attack cooldown and a weapon-rank bonus for deadlier enemies without health sponges
[*][b]NPC flashlights[/b] (experimental): brightness & reach and beam width of the flashlight all 1,600 human NPCs carry, how often they keep it on in combat (by rank) and the hours they switch it on and off. Honest limit: your own flashlight cannot be changed from config files - its light values live in the game's Blueprint assets
[*]Experimental A-Life sliders: max simultaneous NPCs & mutants, spawn distance
[*][b]A-Life spawns (experimental)[/b] - the real spawn controls, found by digging through the lair and director data: LAIR population for mutants and for humans separately (how many live in each place, per player rank), lair respawn speed, and the director's random encounters: frequency, mutant share, pack size, plus a weight per species (blind dogs, boars, fleshes, tushkans, chimeras, mixed mutant packs). Honest limits: story lairs and base guards stay vanilla, bloodsuckers and exotic mutants are never rolled as random encounters in vanilla and the tool does not invent that, and existing saves re-roll lairs slowly (sleep or change region)
[/list]

[b]Factions - its own tab (experimental)[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Your standing with 13 factions[/b] (Loners, Bandits, Military, Ward, Duty, Freedom, Mercenaries, Monolith, Noontide, Spark, Corps, Scientists, and all mutants as one group) on the game's own scale: -800 = enemy / kill on sight, around -400 = wary but talking and trading still work, 0 = neutral, 600+ = friend
[*][b]Faction vs. faction[/b]: expandable blocks with every pairing between the major factions - make Duty and Freedom go to war, or give the bandits friends
[*][b]Reputation rollback time[/b]: how long the game remembers local hostility before forgiving it (vanilla 60 min)
[*][b]Reputation reaction strength[/b]: how hard your deeds hit your reputation - scales every gain and loss in the game's reaction tables (help a faction, kill their members ...) from 25 to 400 %
[*][b]Trading requires standing[/b]: a four-step slider (Enemy / Disaffected / Neutral / Friend) for how much a faction has to like you before its traders deal with you - vanilla is Disaffected, Enemy means everyone trades even with their worst enemy
[*]The tool also raises the game's internal RelationVersion counter - the developers' own mechanism for pushing relation updates into existing saves. [b]Honestly: not verified in-game yet[/b], treat existing saves as untested and keep a backup. Story, boss and arena factions are deliberately not listed, and quests can still override relations at any time (by design)
[/list]

[b]Mutants - its own tab[/b]
[list]
[*]Global sliders: mutant damage, health, speed, hearing - and [b]health regen[/b]: mutants passively regenerate just like human NPCs (44 of 47 prototypes); x0 = wounds stay, the mutant counterpart of 'NPCs don't self-heal'
[*][b]Per-species tree[/b] in four size groups: open a species, set health / speed / damage / regen - each species shows its vanilla numbers (prototype count, HP range, attacks, regen). Damage scales each attack individually. Species only get sliders for things that really work: Poltergeist and rat swarms deal damage indirectly, so no damage slider there - and mutant sight is engine logic, so there is honestly no vision slider at all
[*]Bloodsucker cloaking: cloaking speed, and how hard damage breaks the cloak (x0 = hits never reveal them)
[*][b]Mutant attack cooldown[/b]: the pause between mutant attacks (200 % = half as many attacks)
[/list]

[b]Artifacts & detectors[/b]
[list]
[*]Artifact effect strength, radiation side-effect (0% = clean artifacts), spawn chance
[*]Rare artifact bias: shift rarity rolls toward Uncommon/Rare/Epic at Common's expense
[*][b]Artifacts per anomaly field[/b] (vanilla 1, up to 5 per spawn) and [b]artifact respawn speed[/b]
[*]Detector & scanner range: Echo, Bear, Veles, Gilka, the anomaly beeper and the searchpoint scanner
[/list]

[b]World[/b]
[list]
[*]Anomaly damage - one global slider plus per element type: electro / chemical / fire / gravity
[*]Consumable strength: medkits, bandages, food & drinks (penalties like drunkenness stay vanilla)
[*]Rain & storm frequency, emission frequency
[*][b]Day length[/b] (vanilla: one game day per real hour; up to four hours) and [b]consumable effect duration[/b] (energy drink, Hercules, psy-block ... - instant heals stay instant)
[*][b]Emission duration[/b]: stretch or shorten how long the regular emission takes (25-400 %) - all phases scale together so warning, wave and aftermath keep their vanilla rhythm. Story emissions are deliberately untouched
[*][b]Loot[/b] - two separate systems, four sliders: the smart-loot lists in stashes and on bodies (amount, find chance, ammo bonus), and the item generators behind dead stalkers, mutants and the Zone's stashes (amount). Money, quest items, story rewards, unique weapons and trader stock are excluded by design
[*][b]Dropped weapon condition[/b]: sets the AVERAGE condition of weapons found on bodies and in the world (vanilla ~37.5 %) - the game keeps rolling randomly around it exactly like vanilla, so 80 % gives drops between roughly 67 and 93 %. Tick 'Exact condition' for a fixed value. Armor, helmets, artifacts and trader stock stay vanilla
[/list]

[b]Economy[/b]
[list]
[*]Trader buy/sell prices, per-category price factors (weapons, armor, ammo, artifacts, consumables)
[*]Repair & upgrade costs, quest rewards
[*][b]Technician upgrades[/b] - three checkboxes: take both of mutually exclusive upgrade branches, upgrades need no blueprint, no upgrade tiers (skip the earlier tier). Same route as the 'Take Both Upgrades' / 'Unrestricted Upgrades' mods; which technician services which gear stays vanilla
[*]Repeatable quest cooldown 0-400% (vanilla: task givers wait 24 in-game hours for new jobs; 0% = new jobs right away)
[*]Fast travel cost 0-400% (0% = guides are free)
[/list]

[b]Traders - its own tab[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Stock amount[/b]: how much of everything is on the shelf (ammo boxes, medkit stacks ...)
[*][b]Stock variety[/b]: each catalog item's chance to be in stock after a restock, capped at 100 % - fuller or patchier shelves. Honest limit: the tool never ADDS items to a trader's catalog
[*][b]Restock time[/b] (moved here from Economy) and the minimum durability traders accept when buying your gear
[*][b]Wallets[/b]: a money factor plus an 'unlimited money' checkbox - fun fact from the game data: 59 of 73 traders already have unlimited money in vanilla, so both only touch the 14 finite wallets (bartenders etc.)
[/list]

[b]Tool features[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Mod scan with Avoid-conflicts mode[/b]: on request the tool scans your OTHER installed mods - the ~mods folder AND your Steam Workshop subscriptions (their config part is read; only packed assets stay invisible, and the result says so) - and marks every slider they also change with a colored dot. One checkbox then resets and LOCKS all of them - guaranteed hands-off compatibility, with per-setting unlock buttons. A plain-text compatibility report can be exported from the results
[*][b]Every built pak is an editable preset[/b]: paks embed a manifest with the tool version, build date, game version and ALL settings - 'Load preset ...' accepts .pak files and restores everything
[*][b]'Changed only' view[/b]: one header button dims everything still at (vanilla), so you see your whole mod at a glance
[*][b]Built-in searchable FAQ[/b] ("? FAQ" button): 65 questions, searchable by keywords like "health pack", "carry weight" or "antivirus"
[*][b]DLC checker[/b]: the status line tells you which edition (DLC) content the tool found in your install ("Edition content found (Deluxe, Pre-order, Ultimate): 11 guns, 5 armor pieces.") - or honestly that there is none. Missing edition paks are harmless, the trees simply show base-game content only
[*][b]No internet, at all[/b]: since 1.19.2 the tool contains no networking code - no update check, no downloads, nothing in the background. An indicator next to the FAQ button shows whether the one file you have to supply yourself (the Oodle library) is in place, and a setup window walks you through it
[*][b]Save & load presets[/b] (JSON files in a presets folder next to the exe)
[*][b]Search box[/b] to find any slider - highlights matches, shows which tab, and also finds weapons and ammo rounds by name (opening their category for you)
[*]Reads the vanilla values [b]from YOUR installed game version[/b], so multiplier tweaks stay correct after game patches
[*][b]Only the values you CHANGE get patched[/b] - everything left on "(vanilla)" is untouched, so it won't conflict with your other mods
[/list]

[heading]HOW TO USE (step by step)[/heading]

[list=1]
[*]Download and extract the ZIP
[*]Create a folder, for example on your desktop: Desktop\S2Tweaker
[*]Extract the whole ZIP into that folder - S2Tweaker.exe needs the DLL files and the "_internal" folder that sit next to it
[*]Run S2Tweaker.exe
[*]The tool suggests your game folder, e.g. C:\Games\Steam\steamapps\common\S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2 Heart of Chornobyl - check it. If it's wrong, click "Browse ..." and select your game folder (the one that contains Stalker2\Content\Paks)
[*]Click "Confirm & load game data" and wait 10-20 seconds (only needed on first start and after game updates)
[*]Set your sliders and checkboxes. Everything you leave at "(vanilla)" will NOT be part of the mod
[*]Optional: change the mod name at the bottom
[*]Click "Build pak -> output folder"
[*]Open Desktop\S2Tweaker\output - there is your mod, e.g. zzz_S2Tweaker_P.pak
[*]Copy that file into: <your game folder>\Stalker2\Content\Paks\~mods\
Example: C:\Games\Steam\steamapps\common\S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2 Heart of Chornobyl\Stalker2\Content\Paks\~mods\
If the ~mods folder doesn't exist, create it.
(Shortcut: the "Install to ~mods" button does steps 9-11 in one click and creates the ~mods folder automatically)
[*]Start the game. Done!
[/list]

[heading]CHANGE OR REMOVE THE MOD[/heading]

[list]
[*]Change values: move the sliders, build again - the new pak replaces the old one (same mod name = same file name)
[*]Remove the mod: delete zzz_<YourModName>_P.pak from the ~mods folder, or click "Remove from ~mods" in the tool
[*]Uninstall the tool: just delete your S2Tweaker folder - settings, cache and output all live inside it (fully portable, nothing else is written anywhere)
[/list]

[heading]GOOD TO KNOW[/heading]

[list]
[*]After a game update: start the tool and confirm the game folder again - it detects the new version and re-reads the vanilla values automatically. Then rebuild your pak
[*]If you already use single-purpose mods that change the same values (e.g. "carry weight 200"), remove them - this tool covers them and its pak loads last anyway
[*]Weapon fire rate, spread and recoil live in game data shared with NPCs - NPCs using those weapons are affected too (noted in the app)
[*]Since game patch 2.0 the community reports that cfg-based movement-speed changes can desync from animations or partially not apply - the movement and carry-weight sliders carry an in-app warning until this settles
[*]The fire-rate factor is reported to have the same animation limitation: firing animation and sound can desync from the actual shots. It stays available - test in-game and keep changes moderate
[*]S2Tweaker.exe is the unmodified pythonw.exe from python.org, signed by the Python Software Foundation - that is why it shows the Python icon and Python's version info. If an antivirus tool still flags anything, it is a false positive; the scan links at the top of this page are for exactly the file you download here
[*]Reading the game's packed config files needs Oodle's oo2core_9_win64.dll (0.6 MB). It is proprietary, cannot be shipped with the tool, and the game does not ship it as a separate file either (Oodle is compiled into the game's own executable). Since 1.19.1 the tool does NOT download it - a program that fetches a library and then runs it is exactly what malware does, and that is one reason scanners flag tools like this one. Instead a setup window walks you through getting it once, with pictures, and shows you where to put it. It takes a minute, and afterwards the tool never asks again. You may already have the file: every Unreal Engine installation ships it
[*]Debug checkbox: also exports the raw .cfg patch files to output\<ModName>_cfg so you can see exactly what the mod changes
[/list]

[b]v1.19.2 - No network code, and no archive hidden inside the exe[/b] (~230 tweaks, 13 tabs)
[spoiler]
[list]
[*][b]The executable was 90 % compressed archive - that is fixed.[/b] Shipping as a folder in 1.19.0 only moved the DLLs out; the Python code stayed inside S2Tweaker.exe as a compressed blob of 2,857,284 bytes with an entropy of 7.999 out of a possible 8.0. A small loader in front of a large opaque block is what a packer looks like, and it is how analysts recognise malware built with the same tooling. Nexus' own file guidelines prohibit executables that connect to the internet "unless where it is crucial" and say plainly that "'auto update' functionality does not qualify as crucial" - they were right, so it is out. You can check that claim yourself with one search of the public source
[*][b]To update from now on[/b]: download the new ZIP and replace S2Tweaker.exe and the _internal folder next to it. Your settings, presets, cache and built mods are never touched. The version you are running is in the window title
[*][b]This download is the build server's own output[/b], downloaded from the public GitHub Actions run and uploaded here unchanged - nothing in it was built on my PC
[*]No new game files, no re-extraction. Your existing paks stay valid

[b]v1.19.1 - Nothing is downloaded at runtime any more[/b] (~230 tweaks, 13 tabs)
[spoiler]
[list]
[*][b]The tool no longer fetches anything while it runs.[/b] Until now it downloaded the Oodle decompression library on first use, and the bundled repak could fetch it too. A program that pulls a library off the internet and then executes it is exactly what a dropper does - one of the reasons antivirus scanners kept flagging this tool. That capability is gone now, not disabled
[*][b]repak is built from source[/b] with the download function and its whole HTTP/TLS stack removed. Measured in the binary: 113 occurrences of the TLS library before, none after; the file shrank from 4.4 MB to 2.1 MB. It is also no longer a third-party prebuilt binary, so every file in this download now comes from the public source code, built by a public GitHub Actions workflow
[*][b]You place the Oodle library once, yourself.[/b] A setup window walks you through it with pictures: copy the link, download it in your browser (it may warn about an unverified file - that is normal for a .dll), drop it next to S2Tweaker.exe, restart. It only appears when the file is missing, and two indicators next to the FAQ button show at a glance whether it is there
