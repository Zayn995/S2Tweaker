cfg.bin decoder (s2tweaker/vendor_bin2cfg.py)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Based on bin2cfg.py by joric (https://github.com/joric/stalker/wiki),
building on S2CfgToJSON by sdwvit (https://github.com/sdwvit/S2CfgToJSON)
with the binary reader by thexii. Published as public domain / MIT by their
authors; adapted for S2Tweaker.
